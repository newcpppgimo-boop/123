About the project

SK Digital Engagement Hub

Connecting the Youth. Empowering the Community.

Purpose

This website-only system is an academic/testing prototype for youth engagement in Barangay Santo Rosario Old, Zaragoza, Nueva Ecija.

What it covers

Information, events, youth voice, polls and surveys, notifications, and participation monitoring.

Prototype notice: This system is not an official barangay transaction or government service.
